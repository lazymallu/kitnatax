Salary tax calculator for India, 2026-27

India has two ways to work out salary tax. The new regime has lower rates but lets you subtract almost nothing. The old regime has higher rates but lets you subtract rent, savings and loans. Enter your pay and this page tells you which one costs you less, and whether you get money back. Every rule links to the Income Tax Department's own page.

How each regime gets to your tax

Getting your refund

  1. Get Form 16 from your employer. On the tax portal, download your AIS (a list of all income and tax on your PAN) and check the tax figures match.
  2. If you only have salary, one house and bank interest, use form ITR-1. File by 31 July 2027. Pick the old regime there if it is cheaper for you.
  3. Confirm the return within 30 days with an Aadhaar OTP or net banking. Any refund goes to the bank account you have checked (pre-validated) on the portal.
  4. Made a mistake? You can file a corrected return until 31 March 2028.
