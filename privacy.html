Privacy

Nothing you type into the calculator is sent anywhere. All the arithmetic runs in your browser. Your figures are saved only in your own browser (local storage) so the page remembers them next time. Clearing your browser data removes them.

What we do collect

If site analytics are switched on, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It counts page visits without cookies and without tracking you across sites. It never sees the numbers you type.

Advertising and affiliate links

Some pages may show ads or links to filing services that pay us if you sign up. Ad providers may use cookies; where the law requires it you will be asked first. Affiliate links never change what the calculator tells you.

Fonts

Fonts are served from this site itself. No font service sees your visit.
